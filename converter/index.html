5. Understanding Audio and Video Formats (MP3, MP4, 3GP)

Choosing the right file format is crucial for balancing file size, device compatibility, and media quality. RumusMu supports the industry's most reliable container formats:

MP3 (MPEG-1 Audio Layer III): The universal standard for compressed audio. MP3 files play on virtually every music player, smartphone, and car stereo system on Earth, offering excellent audio fidelity at manageable file sizes.

MP4 (MPEG-4 Part 14): The gold standard for digital video distribution. MP4 containers support advanced video codecs like H.264 alongside AAC audio, delivering crisp high-definition visuals compatible with iOS, Android, Windows, and macOS.

3GP: A simplified video container designed explicitly for mobile devices with limited bandwidth and storage capacity. While less common today, it remains useful for legacy hardware compatibility. To understand container specifications deeper, consult resources on Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) RFC publications.
