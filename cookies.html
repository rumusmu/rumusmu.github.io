1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a smoother browsing experience, and supply website owners with analytical information about site traffic and user behavior.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for several key purposes:

  • To remember your cookie consent preferences and user interface settings;
  • To analyze aggregate traffic patterns and understand how visitors interact with our calculators and articles;
  • To support third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) in serving relevant and personalized advertisements;
  • To maintain the general security and performance of our web infrastructure.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

The cookies utilized on Rumusmu typically fall into the following categories:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for core website functions, such as remembering whether you have accepted or dismissed our cookie consent banner.
  • Analytics & Performance Cookies: Help us measure site traffic, page views, and user journeys so we can continually improve our educational content.
  • Advertising & Targeting Cookies: Placed by our trusted advertising partners (including Google) to track visits and deliver tailored ads across the web.

4. Third-Party and Advertising Cookies

Some cookies on our site may be set by third parties, such as Google AdSense. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to Rumusmu or other websites on the internet. You can learn more about how Google uses data in advertising by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms policy pages.

5. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You have the right to choose whether or not to accept cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website and your user experience.

You can also clear your browser's local storage or adjust your preferences using our cookie banner options when available.

6. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our operational practices. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our use of cookies, please feel free to reach out to our team through our contact channels.
